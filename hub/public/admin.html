Brands

A brand is one ordering website for several outlets (/order/<brand>). The customer gives their location first; a delivery goes to the nearest outlet that is open and delivers there, a pickup to the nearest open outlet. The customer then orders from that outlet's own menu and prices. The website design is the main outlet's (made in its website builder).

Add a brand

Lowercase letters, digits and dashes; not the same as any store's address. Cannot be changed later.
